Understand the major differences between the Old Tax Regime and New Tax Regime before filing your taxes.

Old Tax Regime

Best for people who invest and claim deductions.

Tax Slabs Higher Rates
Deductions Available
80C Benefits Yes
HRA & LTA Available
Best For High Investments
  • Allows deductions under 80C, 80D and HRA.
  • Suitable for salaried employees with investments.
  • Helps reduce taxable income.
  • Useful for LIC, PPF, ELSS and Home Loan benefits.
  • Requires tax planning and document proofs.

New Tax Regime

Best for simple taxation with lower tax rates.

Tax Slabs Lower Rates
Deductions Limited
80C Benefits No
HRA & LTA Not Available
Best For Simple Salaries
  • Offers lower income tax slab rates.
  • Very limited deductions available.
  • Simple and easy tax calculation.
  • No need for heavy investment planning.
  • Suitable for people with fewer exemptions.
