Compare both tax systems and choose the best option for your savings.

Understand the major differences between the Old Tax Regime and New Tax Regime before filing your taxes.

Old Tax Regime

Best for taxpayers who claim deductions and exemptions.

Tax Slabs Higher Rates
Deductions Available
80C Benefits Up to ₹1.5 Lakh
HRA & LTA Available
Best For High Deductions
  • Claim deductions under Section 80C.
  • HRA and LTA exemptions available.
  • Home loan interest deduction allowed.
  • Medical insurance deduction under 80D.
  • Suitable for taxpayers with investments.

New Tax Regime

Best for simple taxation with lower tax rates.

Tax Slabs Lower Rates
Deductions Limited
80C Benefits Not Available
HRA & LTA Not Available
Best For Simple Salaries
  • Offers lower income tax slab rates.
  • Most deductions and exemptions removed.
  • Standard deduction available.
  • Simpler and faster tax filing.
  • Suitable for taxpayers with fewer investments.
