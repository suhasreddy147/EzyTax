Business Tax Guide for Indian Businesses

Understand GST, Import Duty, Export Benefits, TDS, Corporate Tax and Compliance Requirements.

GST

Goods and Services Tax applicable on supply of goods and services.

Registration Limit ₹40 Lakh*
GST Rates 5% - 28%
Returns Monthly/Quarterly
  • Mandatory for eligible businesses.
  • Input Tax Credit available.
  • Inter-state sales require GST compliance.
  • E-invoicing for eligible businesses.

Import Duties

Taxes applicable when importing goods into India.

Basic Customs Duty Applicable
IGST Applicable
Documentation Required
  • Import duties vary by product.
  • GST payable on imported goods.
  • ICEGATE compliance required.
  • Custom clearance documentation mandatory.

Exports

Export goods and services with tax benefits.

GST Zero Rated
Refunds Available
LUT Recommended
  • Exports are generally zero-rated.
  • Claim GST refunds.
  • LUT helps avoid upfront GST payment.
  • Boosts international trade.

Corporate Tax

Income tax applicable on business profits.

Domestic Companies Applicable
TDS Compliance Required
Advance Tax Required
  • Tax based on annual profits.
  • TDS filing required.
  • Advance tax payable quarterly.
  • Annual return filing mandatory.
